# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (Lite) - 2026-11-15

First release of CatchDesk Lite, free and open source under the MIT licence.

- Public enquiry form with up to six photos, a choice of service, urgency, a spam trap and a rate limit.
- Leads inbox with a seven-step pipeline, status tabs, search and leads added by hand.
- Today page with enquiries to answer, jobs to book, quotes waiting and visits coming up.
- Quote builder with lines, quantities, saved services as quick picks, tax (added or included) and an expiry date.
- Customer quote page: accept with a typed name, decline with a reason, print or save as PDF.
- Booking, private notes and a full timeline on each lead.
- Emails for new enquiries, quotes, acceptances, declines and bookings, through Cloudflare Email Sending.
- Team roles with approval for new sign-ups. The first account becomes the admin.
- Private photo storage in R2.
- Demo mode with a sample business, no outgoing email and a nightly reset.
- Starting wording for the Privacy and Terms pages.
- Deploy to Cloudflare button.
